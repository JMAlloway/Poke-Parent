## Bundle A: Starter Explorer

**Title:** Starter Explorer Bundle

**Price:** $54.99

**Description:**

A gentle introduction to Pokémon cards for your child.

This bundle is designed for kids who are just getting started — or for parents who want to test the waters without a big commitment. You'll get a few packs to open together, sleeves to protect your child's favorites, and a small binder to start organizing.

We also include our Parent PDF Guide, which walks you through everything: what the cards are, how to use the supplies, and what to realistically expect when opening packs.

**What's in the box:**
- 4–5 modern booster packs (from current sets)
- 100 penny sleeves
- Small card binder (holds approximately 60 cards)
- Parent PDF guide (emailed after purchase)

**Good to know:**
- Packs are randomized. Most packs won't contain rare cards — that's completely normal.
- Each pack has 10 cards. The fun is in the surprise of seeing what you get.
- The sleeves protect cards from bending, fingerprints, and scratches.
- The binder gives your child a place to organize and display favorites.

## Bundle B: Growing Collector

**Title:** Growing Collector Bundle

**Price:** $84.99

**Description:**

For kids who are already into Pokémon and ready for more.

This bundle gives your child more packs to open, better protection supplies, and a proper binder with side-loading pages (so cards don't fall out). It's our most popular bundle because it hits the sweet spot between value and variety.

The expanded Parent PDF Guide covers everything in the starter guide, plus tips on organizing a growing collection, understanding card types, and knowing when you have "enough."

**What's in the box:**
- 8–10 modern booster packs (from current sets)
- 200 penny sleeves
- 10 semi-rigid top loaders (for their favorite or special cards)
- Card binder with side-loading pages
- Expanded parent PDF guide (emailed after purchase)

**Good to know:**
- More packs means more variety, but packs are still randomized. No guarantees.
- Top loaders are rigid plastic holders — perfect for the 2–3 cards your child is most excited about.
- Side-loading binder pages mean cards won't slide out if the binder tips over.
- The expanded PDF includes a section on understanding card rarity symbols.

More packs, better supplies, and room to grow. Our most popular bundle for kids who are already into Pokémon.
