## Founder Story Section

**Heading:** I'm a dad who didn't grow up with this.

**Body:**

When my kid came home from school talking about Pokémon cards, I had no idea where to start. I walked into a store, stared at a wall of products, and walked out confused and empty-handed.

So I started learning. I figured out what the different packs are, what supplies actually matter, and how to avoid wasting money on things my kid didn't need.

This shop is what I wish existed when I started: a calm, honest place where another parent just explains things plainly. No hype. No jargon. No pressure to spend more than you want to.

Everything here is something I'd buy for my own kid.

— The Poké Parent Shop Founder

## Honesty Promise

**Heading:** Our Honesty Promise

- Packs are randomized. We never guarantee rare cards.
- Cards are for fun and collecting — not financial products.
- Grading is optional and usually unnecessary for kids.
- We'll never pressure you to buy more than your child needs.
- Parental supervision recommended for younger children (small parts in accessories).
