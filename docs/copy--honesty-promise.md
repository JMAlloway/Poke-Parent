## Our Honesty Promise

- **Packs are randomized.** We never guarantee rare cards. Every pack is a surprise — some will have exciting pulls, most will be common cards. That's how it works for everyone.

- **Cards are for fun and collecting — not financial products.** We don't use language like "investing" or "value" because Pokémon cards are a hobby. They're meant to be enjoyed, traded with friends, and organized in binders.

- **Grading is optional and usually unnecessary for kids.** Professional grading exists, but it costs money, takes time, and doesn't magically make cards valuable. For most kids, a penny sleeve and a binder page is all the protection they need.

- **We'll never pressure you to buy more than your child needs.** Our bundles are designed to be complete. You don't need to add anything else unless you want to.

- **Parental supervision recommended for younger children.** Some accessories (sleeves, top loaders) have small parts or rigid edges. We recommend opening packs and handling supplies together with children under 8.

**Supplies (binders, sleeves, top loaders):** Can be returned unused and in original packaging within 30 days.

**Digital products (PDF guides):** Non-refundable, but if you're unhappy with a guide, email us and we'll make it right.
