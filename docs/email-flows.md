## Flow 3: Post-Purchase Follow-Up (3 Days After Delivery)

**Subject:** How did it go? (+ tips for using your supplies)

**Body:**

Hi {{ first_name }},

Your bundle should have arrived by now. We hope opening those packs was fun!

Here are some quick tips for what to do next:

**If your child pulled cards they love:**
Slide them into penny sleeves right away. For their absolute favorites, use a top loader for extra protection. Then put them front and center in the binder.

**If the pulls were "just okay":**
That's normal! Most packs contain common cards. The fun is in the surprise, and every card is still part of a collection. Remind them that even common cards look great in a binder.

**If they want more:**
That's natural too. Before buying more packs, check out our guide on [What to Expect from Packs](/pages/what-to-expect-from-packs). It helps set healthy expectations and avoid the "just one more pack" cycle.

**Binder tips:**
- Fill pages front-to-back to keep weight balanced
- Side-loading pages (included in Growing Collector and up) prevent cards from sliding out
- Store the binder on a shelf, away from direct sunlight

**Need help?** Just reply to this email.

— {{ brand_name }}

P.S. If you found this helpful, we'd love a quick review on our site. It helps other parents find us.
