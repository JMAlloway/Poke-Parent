## Questions

### Q1: How old is the child?
- Under 6 — *Preschool / Kindergarten*
- 6–8 — *Early elementary*
- 9–11 — *Upper elementary*
- 12+ — *Middle school and up*

### Q2: How does your child enjoy Pokémon?
- Playing the card game — *They like battling or want to learn the rules*
- Collecting & organizing — *They love sorting cards, filling binders, finding favorites*
- They just love the characters — *They watch the show or play the video games*
- Not sure yet — *They're interested but haven't started*

### Q3: How careful is your child with their things?
- Rough — *Things get bent, lost, or dropped regularly*
- Mixed — *Careful sometimes, not always*
- Very careful — *They take good care of their belongings*

### Q4: What's their experience with Pokémon cards?
- These would be their first cards
- They have some already
- They've been collecting a while

*Hint: No wrong answer here. All three bundles are good value.*

- $60–$90 — *More packs and better supplies*
